New documents now land in an inbox until they are reviewed, whether they come from an upload, an intake email, an ingestion folder or the API. A document leaves the inbox when you mark it as done, manually add a tag to it, or open it. Organization owners choose between one shared inbox for everyone and one inbox per member in the organization settings, and a "Move to inbox" action puts a document back. The inbox has its own page with the list and grid views, a count badge in the sidebar, and an `e` shortcut in quick look to mark a document as done and move on to the next one.
