Documents now get small preview images generated for PDFs and images, served by a new authenticated endpoint and controlled by the `DOCUMENT_PREVIEWS_ENABLED` option. The documents list shows thumbnails with a hover preview, a new grid view (saved per user, with an optional per-tag override) displays document cards, and a keyboard-friendly quick look overlay (Space on a document) lets you preview, tag and date documents and see search matches without leaving the list.
